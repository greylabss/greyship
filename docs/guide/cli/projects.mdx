List the projects in your team

```text
grey projects
```

Also: `project`.

### `grey projects delete`

Stops the project's apps and deletes it with its deployments, env vars, logs and stored files. This can't be undone. You are asked to type the project's slug first; --yes skips that for scripts.

```text
grey projects delete <slug> [flags]
```

Flags:

```text
      --yes   don't ask for confirmation
```
