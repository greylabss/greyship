Shows your notification settings. Change them with grey notifications email|push on|off [event], where event is deploy.succeeded, deploy.failed or project.created (all of them when left out). Browser push is turned on in the dashboard.

```text
grey notifications
```

### `grey notifications email`

Turn email notifications on or off

```text
grey notifications email on|off [event]
```

### `grey notifications push`

Turn push notifications on or off

```text
grey notifications push on|off [event]
```
